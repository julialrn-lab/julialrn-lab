# Hi, I'm Julia 👋

Mathematics, Chemistry and Science teacher in the São Paulo State public school system,
with an MSc in Materials Science and Technology (POSMAT/UNESP). I work at the intersection of
**organic chemistry, optical chemosensors and data analysis**, and I am pursuing a
Bachelor's in Information Technology at UNIVESP.

## 🔬 Research interests
- Synthesis of quinoline derivatives by multicomponent reactions
- Colorimetric and fluorescent chemosensors for metal ions and pH
- Python for spectroscopic data and sensor arrays (PCA, pattern recognition)

## 🛠️ Tools
- **Python** (pandas) applied to my own spectroscopy data — currently learning Matplotlib and scikit-learn
- **Git/GitHub**
- **SQL** — getting started
- **UV-Vis** and **fluorescence spectroscopy**, **NMR**, **FTIR**
